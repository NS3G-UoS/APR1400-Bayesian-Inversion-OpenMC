GroupKFold balances folds by size, so the alpha-grouped 10-fold CV is unbalanced: fold 0 removes alpha level(s) 1.100 entirely (22 held-out rows, 43 training rows); fold 1 removes alpha level(s) 0.400 entirely (18 held-out rows, 47 training rows). These 2 folds contain 40 of 65 holdouts (62%), each tested after its whole alpha stack was removed from training. The other 8 folds hold out only 3-4 rows each. No alpha level appears in both training and test data in any fold (leakage-free), but this is a harsh and unbalanced test and should be reported as such.
